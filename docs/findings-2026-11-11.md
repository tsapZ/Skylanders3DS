# Findings 2026-11-11: the portal's commands (reply table experiments)

Giants 3DS at "Aim at the PORTAL OF POWER", Azahar with the portal patch, replies from a table
(SKYLANDERS_PORTAL_REPLIES). Every packet the game sends starts with `ff ff ff`; the rest is the console
portals' command set.

| Try | Table | What the game sent next |
|---|---|---|
| none | (silent) | `52 00` (R) three times 300 ms apart, disconnect, again |
| A | R -> `52 02 1b` (a USB Trap Team portal's answer) | accepted: `41 01` (A: activate), `43 ff ff ff` (C: the portal light white) four times, `41 01`, `43 ff ff ff` three times, `41 00` (A: deactivate) twice, disconnect, again |
| B | A, then also A -> `41 <flag> ff 77` (the USB portal's echo) | accepted: after `41 00` it activates again (`41 01`) and asks `53` (S: the status) three times, then disconnects. Unlike a USB portal, which sends its status by itself, the 3DS game asks for it |
| C | B, then also S -> `53 00 00 00 00 00 01` (no figure, counter 0, active; skylanders-portal's status layout) | **the game takes the portal**: the screen goes from "Aim at the PORTAL OF POWER" to "Place a toy on the PORTAL OF POWER", and the connection stays (676 packets in one session): S four times, then R, A, C, A again, round and round |
| D | C, with S saying "slot 0: just put on" (03) and every block of a figure for Q, all fixed | no Q: with the counter always 00 the game seems to wait for a new status, and re-inits after four |
| E | the device answers itself, as skylanders-portal's SoftwarePortal (a counter in S, "just put on" for 8 reports, then "on"; Q from a figure file: a kit-made Sonic Boom) | **the game reads the figure**: after two S it sends `51 20 00` (Q, block 0), gets it, sets the light (`43 00 00 5b`, a dark blue) and asks for block 1 |

**The three bytes in front of every command are the portal light's colour.** They are `ff ff ff` (white) until
the game sets a colour with C; after `43 00 00 5b` every packet starts with `00 00 5b`. So an infrared packet is
`<r> <g> <b> <command ...>`: the portal gets its light with every packet (it has no USB to keep it lit). In try E the
device only skipped ff bytes, so the read of block 1 (`00 00 5b 51 10 01`) went unanswered; now it always skips
three.

| Try | Device | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| E2 | E, the colour skipped | **the figure arrives**: the game reads blocks 0, 1, 8, 0x24, 0x11, 0x2d, 0x25 .. 0x2c (the light pulsing blue meanwhile: `00 00 e0` .. `00 00 42`), shows the Skylander in a crystal ("Now choose another Skylander to store in a second crystal. The Portaltron allows you to keep two here at the same time!"), then disconnects and connects again; with the same figure still there: "You must summon two different Skylanders into the game" |

Reads seen so far: `51 <x0> <block>` with x = 1, 2 or 3 in the high nibble (2 for the first block, 3 now and then;
maybe a retry or sequence mark), answered with `51 10 <block> <16 bytes>` all the same. No W (write) yet.
The device now takes several figure files (SKYLANDERS_PORTAL_FIGURE, separated by ';'): after the game has read
one and reconnects, the next stands on the portal. tools/make_blank_figure.py makes empty ones (byte for byte as
skylanders-portal's CreateBlankFigure, checked against a kit-made figure).

So the game takes a console portal's R answer as it is (no `ff ff ff` in front of the reply), activates
the portal and sets its light, the same commands as on Xbox 360; it gives up when A gets no answer.

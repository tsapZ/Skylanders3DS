## The portal

The 3DS games came with their own Portal of Power: battery-powered (3 AA), talking to the console
over the infrared port on the console's back, switching itself off after 30 s without infrared
(Activision's support FAQ for SuperChargers Racing). The figures are the console ones, so the
figure files and the catalog in skylanders-portal hold what the portal reads; what goes over the
infrared is the unknown part.

No emulation of it was found: an emulation wiki line credits the AzaharPlus fork with "support for
the Skylanders IR portal on desktop", but its repository (f4mrfaux/AzaharPlus) and mainline Azahar
have no portal code. The one "Skylanders" in Azahar (`nfc_types.h`) is the amiibo series ID.

No write-up of the infrared protocol was found either (searched 2026-11-11). One hardware note: a
hobbyist who opened a Giants 3DS portal lists a PIC microcontroller and an NXP MFRC530 reader chip
inside ([Project Pokemon forum thread](https://projectpokemon.org/home/forums/topic/28225-emulating-wifi-subdiscussion-of-pkx-the-new-pokemon-format-for-gen-6/page/2)),
a MIFARE reader like the console portals'. So the infrared side may well carry the same kind of
commands (activate, query a slot, read a block) in its own framing; the log of what the game sends
(the patched ir:USER device) will tell.

## Azahar 2126.2 (official Windows build), hands-on

- Portable: a `user` folder next to `azahar.exe`; settings: volume 0, no update check on start,
  no close prompt, log filter `*:Info Service.IR:Trace`.
- Spyro's Adventure 3DS: boots to its language screen (10 languages), OpenGL, 60 FPS, 100 % speed.
- Giants 3DS: boots to the title and the profile screen; New Game, Play, then A through the first
  dialog, and the game asks "Aim at the PORTAL OF POWER — Don't block the infrared transceiver".
- Taps on the touch screen and keys work when posted to Azahar's window (WM_LBUTTONDOWN/UP and
  WM_KEYDOWN/UP to the child window under the point); the keyboard's A is the 3DS A button.

## Azahar's ir:USER (src/core/hle/service/ir)

- `IRDevice`: `OnConnect()`, `OnDisconnect()`, `OnReceive(data)` (what the game sends with
  `SendIrNop`), `Send(data)` (to the game: `IR_USER::PutToReceive` wraps it into the packet the
  game reads from shared memory: A5, network ID, size, payload, CRC-8 with polynomial 0x07).
- One device so far: `ExtraHID`, the Circle Pad Pro / New 3DS buttons, connected by
  `RequireConnection` (0x0006) with device ID 1, which sets connection_status 2, connection_role 2,
  connected 1 in the shared memory header and signals the connection event.
- `AutoConnection` (0x0007), `AnyConnection` (0x0008), `WaitConnection` (0x0005) and
  `GetConnectionStatus` (0x0013) are empty entries in the function table.
- `SendIrNop` logs the game's data at Trace level: with AutoConnection connected, the log shows the
  portal protocol's first bytes.

## The protocol, first look (2026-11-11, step 2 done)

Azahar built from source (tag 2126.2) with the portal patch (emulator/apply_portal_patch.py), Giants 3DS
through the profile, the intro and the first dialog to "Aim at the PORTAL OF POWER" (tools/azahar_taps.ps1,
A every 8 s from 150 s on):

- At the prompt the game calls AutoConnection (0xff, then 3000000, 3694000, 11082000, 13302000, 13302000:
  probably timeouts in ticks) and, once connected, sends the same 5 bytes three times, about 300 ms
  apart: `ff ff ff 52 00`. Without an answer it disconnects after the third, connects again at once and
  starts over; 334 such packets in about 75 s.
- 0x52 is 'R', the console portals' reset/ready command, which a USB portal answers with 'R' and two
  bytes naming the portal (52 02 1b for a Trap Team portal, 52 01 xx for older ones). So the infrared
  portal very likely speaks the console command set, with three 0xff bytes in front (a preamble, or
  the infrared side's own header) and a 0x00 after.
- Next: the device answers from a table of hex replies (SKYLANDERS_PORTAL_REPLIES, a text file of
  "<request prefix> => <reply>" lines, read again for every packet) to find the answer the game takes;
  then the next command it sends shows how far it gets.
